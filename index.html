Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 1, 2026

1. Information You Enter

Expense Tracker allows you to create and manage expense records and categories. Depending on how you use the App, information stored locally may include:

  • Expense titles
  • Expense amounts
  • Expense dates
  • Expense notes
  • Expense categories
  • Categories you create

2. Your Expense Data

In the current version of Expense Tracker, your expense data is not uploaded to our servers or stored in a cloud database.

The App operates using local storage on your device.

3. Advertising

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process certain information from your device for purposes such as delivering advertisements, measuring advertising performance, and preventing fraud and abuse.

For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy .

4. Personal Information

Expense Tracker does not require you to create an account in the current version. We do not require your name, email address, or telephone number to use the core expense-tracking functionality.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your expense records. Your locally stored expense information is not transmitted to our servers in the current version.

6. Data Security

Because your expense information is stored locally, the security of that information also depends on the security of your device.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Your expense records are stored locally on your device until you delete them or remove the App, subject to the behavior of your device's operating system and backup mechanisms.

8. Children's Privacy

Expense Tracker is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

The current version may use third-party services necessary to provide advertising and related functionality.
