## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to **Expense Tracker**, an Android application developed and published by **Karthik S** ("we", "us", or "our").

This policy explains what information the App stores, how information is handled, and what third-party services may process information when you use the App.

## 2. Information You Enter

Expense Tracker allows you to create and manage expense records and categories. Depending on how you use the App, information stored locally on your device may include:

- Expense titles
- Expense amounts
- Expense dates
- Expense notes
- Expense categories
- Categories you create

## 3. Local Storage of Expense Data

In the current version, your expense records are stored locally on your device. We do not upload your expense records to our own servers or store them in our own cloud database.

Your locally stored expense information remains on your device unless you delete it or it is otherwise removed through device or operating-system behavior.

## 4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain device information, advertising identifiers, usage information, and diagnostic data, as applicable, to provide advertisements, measure advertising performance, and prevent fraud and abuse.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and practices.

For more information, see [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and the [Google AdMob information page](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543).

## 5. Personal Information and Accounts

The current version of Expense Tracker does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, or telephone number to use its core expense-tracking features.

This does not mean that third-party advertising services necessarily collect no information. Their processing is described in the relevant third-party privacy policies.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your locally stored expense records.

The App does not transmit your expense records to our own servers in the current version. Third-party services, including advertising providers, may process information as described in this policy and their own policies.

## 7. Data Security

Expense information is stored locally on your device. Its security also depends on your device's security settings, operating system, and any backup services you use.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your expense records remain stored locally until you delete them or remove the App, subject to device behavior and operating-system backup mechanisms.

If your device backs up application data, copies may remain in those backups according to your device or backup provider's retention policies.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Expense Tracker is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. Third-party advertising services may have their own requirements and safeguards concerning children's data.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will revise the Effective Date at the top of this document.
